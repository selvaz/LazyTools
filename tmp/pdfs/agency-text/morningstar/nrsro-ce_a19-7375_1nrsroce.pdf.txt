ensure consistency across policies related to Credit Rating withdrawals. 
7/3/2018 Exhibit 2 was updated because the MCR Policy Glossary was created to centralize all 
defined terms in MCR's policies and procedures.  Some definitions were enhanced to 
provide clarity and new terms were added. Exhibit 3 was updated because the 
Confidential Information and Analytical Firewall Policy was updated to reflect MCR's 
business divisions. Also, the Code of Ethics was updated in Exhibits 3, 5 and 7 to reflect 
a change in the definition of Access Person.   Exhibit 4 was updated to include updates 
to the MCR Org Chart to reflect organizational structural changes.  Exhibit 7 was 
updated because the Look Back Review Policy was updated to align the language with 
that of the post-employment certification form and to expand the scope of what MCR 
considers to be participating in determination of credit ratings 
7/23/2018 Exhibit 9 was updated to reflect the change in DCO wh en Candace Chen was appointed 
as MCR's Chief Compliance Officer. 
8/28/2018 Exhibit 2 was updated when the General Description of  the Credit Rating Process was 
updated to add a summary of how assessments of the quality of originators of assets 
of certain types of structured securities factor into the determination of Credit Ratings; 
The MCR Policy Glossary was removed from Exhibit 2. Exhibit 4 was updated to reflect 
an organizational structural change with the appointment of the Head of Surveillance.
